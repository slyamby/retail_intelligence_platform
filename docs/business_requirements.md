# Business Requirements

## Business Objective

Reduce the time required to prepare monthly sales reports while improving data quality and providing management with timely insights.

---

## Current Challenges

- Sales data comes from multiple branches.
- CSV files have inconsistent formatting.
- Duplicate transactions occur.
- Missing values require manual correction.
- Product names are inconsistent.
- Reports are prepared manually.
- Management receives reports late.

---

## Functional Requirements

The system shall:

1. Import CSV files automatically.
2. Combine all branch data.
3. Remove duplicate records.
4. Handle missing values.
5. Standardize date formats.
6. Standardize product names.
7. Validate numeric fields.
8. Produce a clean master dataset.
9. Generate an interactive dashboard.
10. Forecast future sales.

---

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Easy to refresh monthly
- Easy to maintain
- Fast processing
- Accurate results
- User-friendly interface

---

## Expected Business Benefits

- Reduce report preparation time
- Improve decision making
- Reduce manual work
- Increase reporting accuracy
- Improve inventory planning
