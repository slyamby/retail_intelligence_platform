## 1. The Problem We Are Solving
Every month, your business exports critical sales data that arrives in a fragmented, unstandardized, and "messy" format. Currently, valuable hours are wasted manually formatting columns, fixing missing entries, deduplicating records, and forcing data into matching categories.
This manual intervention introduces three major operational bottlenecks:

* Time Drain: Hours spent editing spreadsheets manually instead of analyzing performance or driving revenue.
* Human Error: Manual data entry runs a high risk of accidental deletions, formatting inconsistencies, or missed figures.
* Delayed Insights: Decisions are bottlenecked because insights are trapped behind hours of tedious preparation.

------------------------------
## 2. What the Solution Will Do
We will engineer a custom, automated Sales Data Processing Pipeline using Python. Instead of a human manually correcting spreadsheets line-by-line, a lightweight script will handle the heavy lifting instantly.
When you export your raw monthly sales data, you will simply drop it into a folder and run the script. The program will automatically:

* Clean and Standardize: Isolate raw inputs, drop completely missing rows, and flag text variations.
* Map Information Instantly: Automatically translate mismatched raw records (like unmapped payment methods or regions) into clean, standardized business categories.
* Isolate Outliers: Flag any brand-new, unrecognized data points (such as a new payment type) and isolate them into a clean report so your system stays up to date without crashing.
* Format & Compile: Merge all processed information and format it perfectly for your business tools.
